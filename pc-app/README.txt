PhoneRemote - control this PC from your phone
=============================================

1. Double-click "Start PhoneRemote.bat".
2. A browser tab opens. Click Start, choose "Entire screen", click Share.
3. On your phone, scan the QR code (or open the link shown and type the code + PIN).

No settings to change and nothing to install. Keep the black window
and the browser tab open while you use it; close the black window to stop.
Next time your phone reconnects by itself - just do steps 1 and 2.

Phone controls: tap = click, hold = right-click, drag = drag, two fingers = scroll,
pinch = zoom. Switch to Trackpad mode in the bottom bar for precise pointing.
